# Twelve Favourite Problems

**Purpose**: Forte's capture filter, borrowed from Richard Feynman. A standing
list of the open questions you care about, so you know what to capture.
**When**: once now, then revisit each quarter.
**Inputs**: your notes, goals and recent captures.

```text
Act as a thinking partner. I want to write my "twelve favourite problems":
open-ended questions I keep returning to, phrased so that anything I read,
watch or hear can be tested against them.

Here is context about me:
<context>
{{context_about_goals_work_health_faith_projects}}
</context>

Draft 12 questions. Each must:
- start with "How might I", "What would it take to", or "Why does"
- be specific enough to recognise a relevant idea, broad enough to last a year
- cover a spread: work and craft, business and money, health and recovery,
  faith and character, relationships, systems and tools, creativity

Then, for each question, give one example of a capture that would answer
part of it, drawn from my context. Output as a numbered Markdown list.
```

**Output**: save as `30_Resources/twelve-favourite-problems.md` and pin it in
Todoist as a note.
**Source**: Tiago Forte, *Building a Second Brain*, chapter on Capture
(paraphrased); Feynman via Forte.
