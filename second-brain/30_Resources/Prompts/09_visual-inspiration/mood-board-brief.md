# Mood Board Brief (architecture, manufacturing, brand)

**Purpose**: write the brief that a mood board, Sketchup concept, Firefly
generation or Lovable page should answer. Serves Toop Street, houseboat and
dam ideas, Morley Studio fit-out, Hydralada rebrand.
**When**: at the start of any visual project.
**Inputs**: `{{project}}`, `{{site_or_product}}`, `{{constraints}}`,
`{{references}}` (pins or folders), `{{feeling}}`.

```text
Project: {{project}}
Subject: {{site_or_product}}
Constraints (budget, council, materials, brand rules): {{constraints}}
References I have collected: {{references}}
How it should feel in three words: {{feeling}}

Write a mood board brief in Markdown:
1. Intent: one paragraph on what the design must do for people using it.
2. Palette: 5 colours with hex codes and where each is used.
3. Materials: 4 to 6, each with why it fits the constraints and the feeling.
4. Forms and proportions: 3 rules (for example: horizontal emphasis, 31.15
   degree slant echoing the Hydralada mark, one bold material per elevation).
5. Light: natural and artificial, two lines.
6. Precedents: from my references only, name 3 and what to borrow from each.
7. Anti-references: 3 things to avoid.
8. Generation prompts: 3 image prompts for Firefly or similar, 40 to 60 words
   each, consistent with the above.
9. Deliverables: what the mood board, concept sketch and model should show.
```

**Output**: `10_Projects/<project>/mood-brief.md`.
**Source**: original.
