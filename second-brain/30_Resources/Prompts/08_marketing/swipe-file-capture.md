# Swipe File Capture (marketing material)

**Purpose**: capture any piece of marketing that caught your eye (ad, email,
landing page, brochure, post, packaging) as a structured swipe-file entry
with what to steal and where to use it.
**When**: whenever something good crosses your screen. Keep it under two
minutes per entry.
**Inputs**: `{{material}}` (pasted copy, OCR of a screenshot, or a
description), `{{source}}`, `{{brand}}`.

```text
Add this to my marketing swipe file.

Source: {{source}}
Brand: {{brand}}
Material:
<material>
{{material}}
</material>

Return Markdown with:
- TITLE: brand plus format plus the hook in 8 words
- FORMAT: ad, email, landing page, brochure, social post, video, packaging, other
- HOOK: the first line or visual that earned attention, quoted exactly
- STRUCTURE: the sequence it uses (for example: problem, agitation, proof,
  offer, CTA), as a numbered list with one line each
- WHAT WORKS: three specific techniques, each tied to a quoted element
- WHAT I WOULD CHANGE: one line
- STEAL FOR: which Hydralada product line or personal project could use this
  structure (Horticulture, Viniculture, Industrial, Arjes, Bandit, Pellenc,
  Tow and Blow, Mango, NEXUS, mindfulmomentum.tech, Toop Street), and a
  one-line sketch of the adapted hook
- TAGS: 4 to 6 tags
- FILE TO: 30_Resources/Marketing-Swipe-File/<format>/
```

**Output**: one note per item; the Marketing Capture agent batches them.
**Source**: original.
