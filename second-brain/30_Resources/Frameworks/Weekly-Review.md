# The Weekly Review as an Operating System

Source: Tiago Forte,
https://fortelabs.com/blog/the-weekly-review-is-an-operating-system/
Paraphrased and adapted to Brad's tools.

Forte's point: the weekly review is not a chore list, it is the operating
system that keeps every other habit running. Do it on the same day each
week. Decide, do not do: the review is for routing items, not for working on
them.

## Part I: Emergency triage

1. **Clear email inboxes** (Gmail, Outlook). Oldest to newest. Each email goes
   to one of four places: calendar (Morgen), tasks (Todoist), reference
   (`30_Resources` or Notion), or read-later. Touch each once.
2. **Check the calendar, minus two weeks and plus four.** Backwards for
   follow-ups you owe. Forwards for preparation you need to schedule.

## Part II: Preventative maintenance

3. **Clear physical inbox and Remarkable.** Photograph or export pages worth
   keeping into `00_Inbox`. Capture open loops as Todoist tasks. Mark pages
   processed.
4. **Clear desktop and downloads** on every machine. Each file: delete, file
   under PARA, or capture a task about it. Empty the bin.
5. **Check transactions** (bank, Xero, subscriptions). Categorise, look for
   surprises, cancel what you do not use. This is where the Lovable, MyClaw
   and HitPaw renewals in your Inbox would have been caught.

## Part III: Planning action

6. **Process `00_Inbox` into PARA.** Expect 30 to 50 notes. File each by
   actionability: project, area, resource, archive.
7. **Clarify new tasks.** For every new Todoist item: what is the next
   physical action, which project or area, what priority. Delete or archive
   anything you will never do.
8. **Review Waiting-For.** Chase what you are owed. Label tasks
   `@waiting-for` so the list builds itself.
9. **Choose this week's focus.** First the must-dos by urgency across all
   projects. Then from two or three focus projects, the most important items.
   Move those into Todoist "This week" or time-block them in Morgen.

## Adaptation for an AI-run review

The Weekly Review agent (`agents/10-weekly-review-agent.md`) can do steps 6
to 9 against Todoist and this repository automatically and leave you a
one-page summary to approve. Steps 1 to 5 stay human because they touch live
accounts and physical things.
