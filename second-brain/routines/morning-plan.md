# Morning Plan (daily, 5 minutes)

1. Dictate for two minutes: how you slept, what is on your mind, what would
   make today a win. Save as `00_Inbox/_dictation/YYYY-MM-DD_am.txt` (Whisper
   Flow hotkey, or TalkerACR export).
2. The Journal Agent runs Morning Pages, reads today's calendar and flagged
   tasks, and writes `20_Areas/Journal/YYYY/YYYY-MM-DD.md` with One Thing,
   three supporting tasks and time blocks.
3. You: read it, adjust, drag the blocks into Morgen. Start the One Thing
   before opening email.

Manual fallback: paste the dictation into the prompt at
`30_Resources/Prompts/06_journaling/morning-pages.md`.
