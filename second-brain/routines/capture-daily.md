# Capture Daily (06:37)

The Capture Agent processes everything new in Todoist Inbox and the
`01 Capture` projects, Remarkable exports and dictation into clean notes in
`00_Inbox`. You do nothing except keep capturing all day:

- Todoist quick add (phone widget) for tasks and thoughts.
- Pinterest save for visual inspiration (synced Saturday).
- Browser: send to Todoist or bookmark into a `00_Inbox` folder.
- WhatsApp: forward to yourself, export weekly.
- Remarkable: write; export at the weekly review.

Resonance test before capturing: does it surprise, move, or serve a project?
If not, let it go.
