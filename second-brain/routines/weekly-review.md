# Weekly Review (Sunday, 30 minutes)

Agent: `agents/10-weekly-review-agent.md` runs first and leaves you
`30_Resources/Reviews/YYYY-Www_weekly-review.md`. You then do the human half.

## Human checklist (Forte steps 1 to 5)

- [ ] Gmail and Outlook to zero: calendar, task, reference, read-later. Touch once.
- [ ] Calendar: look back two weeks for follow-ups, forward four weeks for prep.
- [ ] Remarkable: export the week's pages to `00_Inbox/_dictation/` (or OCR text).
- [ ] Desktop and Downloads on Zbook, Lenovo and Toop PC: delete, file to PARA, or capture a task.
- [ ] Transactions and subscriptions: categorise, cancel what you do not use, note renewals.

## Agent half (steps 6 to 9), review and approve

- [ ] Read the routing table; approve or correct folder moves.
- [ ] Read clarified tasks; paste approved ones into Todoist.
- [ ] Chase the waiting-for list.
- [ ] Confirm this week's focus in `routines/focus.md` (remove PROPOSED).
- [ ] Time-block the three outcomes in Morgen.

## Close

- [ ] Journal Agent reflection ran (or dictate three lines and run it).
- [ ] One line pinned on the wall for the week.
